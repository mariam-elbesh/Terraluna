## 🌌 Overview

This digital platform offers detailed insights into terrestrial locations that mimic lunar and Martian landscapes, serving as a comprehensive database for global space agencies.

By highlighting these unique planetary analog regions on Earth, the project facilitates:
* Critical Scientific Research: Studying Martian and lunar-like geological and environmental conditions.
* Equipment Testing & Robotic Simulations: Testing rovers, sensors, and exploration hardware in realistic terrains.
* Human Qualification & Habitat Planning: Supporting astronaut survival training and the future construction of sustainable planetary bases.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* Clear & Organized Interface: Presents complex planetary analog data in an accessible, user-friendly format.
* Similarity Percentage Metrics: Displays a calculated percentage for each Earth location indicating its degree of similarity to lunar or Martian environments.
* Verified Scientific Citations: Every listed region is backed by documented, credible scientific references.
